Privacy Policy for BetterTrack

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Miguel Macias built BetterTrack as a free utility app for track and field coaches and athletes.

Summary

BetterTrack does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Written notes and to-do items
  • Saved calculator results
  • Stopwatch folders, athlete names, and lap times
  • Workout builder sessions

Data does not leave your device unless you use the iOS Share sheet to export text.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or login
  • No servers — works fully offline
  • No analytics, ads, or tracking
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data
  • No third-party data-collection SDKs

Questions? Email bettertrackcalculations@gmail.com.
